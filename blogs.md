## 1. How to Gain SEO, AEO & GEO: A Developer's Guide to Ranking in Google and AI Engines

- **Status:** idea
- **Description:** A practical breakdown of the three optimization disciplines —
  classic SEO (rank for clicks), AEO (be extracted by answer engines), and GEO
  (be cited by generative AI). Cover SSR/static rendering, structured data
  (`ProfilePage`, `SoftwareSourceCode`, `FAQPage`), `llms.txt` / `llms-full.txt`,
  answer-first writing, and the Reddit/GitHub authority loop — using this very
  portfolio as the worked example.
- **Primary keywords:** SEO vs AEO vs GEO, generative engine optimization,
  answer engine optimization, llms.txt, AI search visibility.
- **Languages:** EN (later: FR, AR).
